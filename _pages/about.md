<div class="profile-intro" markdown="1">

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the [Machine Learning Department](https://www.ml.cmu.edu) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu), advised by [Prof. Christos Faloutsos](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~christos/) and [Prof. Geoffrey J. Gordon](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ggordon/). I am also a part-time Visiting Researcher with Meta AI's Modern Recommendation Systems team.

<h2 class="profile-research-heading">Research</h2>

<ul class="research-points">
  <li><strong>Geometric learning:</strong> geometry as a modeling principle for geometric deep learning, graph representation learning, and language and foundation models.</li>
  <li><strong>Structures and methods:</strong> measuring and encoding structure in networks, recommender systems, text, and reasoning using Riemannian, spectral, and mixed-curvature methods.</li>
  <li><strong>Language-model geometry:</strong> studying geometry across the model lifecycle—from pretraining and continued adaptation to retrieval and inference—to understand and improve capabilities and computational behavior.</li>
</ul>

<p class="profile-background">Previously, I worked with <a href="https://www.amazon.science/locations/san-francisco-bay-area">Amazon Science</a>, <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/">Microsoft Research India</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com">LinkedIn AI</a> on graph learning, self-supervision, misinformation, and large-scale incident analysis. I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at <a href="https://www.iiitd.ac.in/">IIIT Delhi</a> in 2023.</p>
